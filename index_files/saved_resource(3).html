1
Go to Claude. Open claude.ai in your browser, or download the app from claude.com/download.
2
Sign up or log in. You can use a Google account or an email. The free plan works for most of these — a paid plan unlocks longer chats and more usage.
3
Start a new chat. Click "New chat" and you'll see a message box at the bottom of the screen.
4
Paste and go. Come back here, pick a command below, fill in your details, and copy the generated prompt. Paste it into the Claude message box and press Enter.
